Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the website at everyrealestatecalculator.com ("the website") handles information. It covers the public website only. RealtorCRM is a separate application with its own sign-up and its own data practices.

Calculators run in your browser

Every calculator on this website runs entirely in your web browser. The figures you enter are processed on your device by the page's own code. The website's code does not send your calculator inputs to a server, does not save them, and does not use browser storage (such as localStorage) or cookies to keep them. When you close or leave a page, the figures you entered are gone unless you saved them yourself.

Information the website itself collects

The website has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no comment or upload features. The contact page helps you prepare a message draft in your browser; it does not send or store that draft on the website. The website's own code does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising scripts.

Hosting and content-delivery logs

Like almost every website, this site is served through a hosting provider and a content-delivery network. Those providers automatically receive technical information needed to deliver pages — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — and may keep standard server logs for security and operations. That logging is controlled by the providers, not by the website's own code. [Owner to confirm provider log retention before publication.]

External links

Some pages link to official third-party resources, such as the IRS and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. When you follow those links you leave this website, and the other site's privacy policy applies.

Advertising and affiliates

The website does not currently display advertising or use affiliate tracking. If that changes, this policy will be updated before those features are activated.

Children

This website is a set of financial planning tools for adults and is not directed at children.
