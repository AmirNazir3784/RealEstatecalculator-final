Before you begin

A few useful answers.

What to expect from the free tools and RealtorCRM.

Are the calculators free to use?

Yes. All ten calculators are free to use on this website, with no signup. RealtorCRM is a separate product; it is free during early access.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or signup is required for the current calculators. Open a tool, enter your figures and review the result.

How should I use the estimates?

Use them to explore scenarios, not as a lender quote or a final earnings figure. The mortgage estimate covers principal and interest, plus property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA dues and PMI for any of those costs you enter; a cost left at zero stays out of the total. The commission estimate excludes taxes and costs beyond the fee you enter. Both use USD; check the assumptions on each tool page.

How is RealtorCRM different from the free tools?

The calculators answer specific number-based questions. RealtorCRM is the flagship CRM for ongoing contacts, deals and follow-ups. Explore its capabilities and access availability.
