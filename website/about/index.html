How the calculators work

Every calculator on this site uses a transparent formula or method, shown on its own page, with its assumptions stated and a worked example you can check by hand. The examples use illustrative figures entered on the page — not live lender quotes, market feeds or tax-authority data — and each page explains what its estimate leaves out.
