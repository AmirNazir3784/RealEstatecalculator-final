All ten calculators, in plain language

Every calculator below runs free in your browser, shows its formula or method, and includes a worked example you can check by hand. Use the search and filters above for the card view, or browse the full list here.

Buying

  • Mortgage Calculator

    Work out the full monthly payment on a home loan: principal and interest, plus property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA dues and PMI for the costs you enter. For buyers and owners comparing loan terms — the total payment matters more than the loan payment alone.

  • Home Affordability Calculator

    Start from the monthly payment you can carry and solve backwards to a top home price, using your income, debts and down payment. For buyers who want a budget before they start shopping.

  • Rent vs Buy Calculator

    Compare the full multi-year cost of renting against buying, including equity, selling costs and the year buying pulls ahead. For anyone deciding whether a move is worth it yet — or at all.

Investing

  • Cap Rate Calculator

    Compare a rental property's net operating income with its price or value as a percentage — its income yield before financing. For investors screening deals; because it ignores the mortgage, it compares properties rather than loans.

  • NOI Calculator

    Add up a property's effective rental income and subtract its operating expenses — never the mortgage — to get net operating income per year and per month. The figure behind cap rate, income-based valuations and lender reviews.

  • Property Investment Calculator

    Model one rental end to end: monthly cash flow, cash-on-cash return, cap rate, DSCR and the rent at which the deal breaks even. For landlords checking whether a specific property actually pays.

  • ROI Calculator

    Project a rental's whole-hold return — cash flow plus appreciation plus loan paydown — as a total percentage and an annualized rate. For investors comparing holding a property against selling it or choosing a different deal.

Selling

  • Bridge Loan Calculator

    Estimate how much you can borrow against your current home to buy the next one first, the interest-only cost while the two homes overlap, and the cash left after your sale settles. For movers who need to buy before they sell.

Tax

  • Capital Gains Tax Calculator

    Estimate the federal tax on selling a property: the gain, the primary-residence exclusion, depreciation recapture and your net proceeds after tax. For sellers and investors planning a sale — an estimate to plan with, not a tax filing.

For agents

  • Commission Calculator

    Turn a sale price and commission rate into the amount that actually reaches the agent after the brokerage split and transaction fees, with the seller's total commission cost shown too. For agents forecasting income and sellers checking what a sale costs.
